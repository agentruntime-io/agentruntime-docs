**Autopilot** is the AI assistant built into the Console. You type what you want in plain language, and it can answer questions about your workspace or do the work for you, such as creating a workflow or starting a run.

This page is for anyone using the Console. You do not need any setup beyond signing in.

## Open the assistant

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the chat panel">
    In the [Console](https://console.agentruntime.io), click the speech-bubble icon, **Open chat**, at the right end of the top bar. It is the last of three icons: the bell (notifications), a gauge (credits) and the speech bubble. None of them has a text label. The chat panel opens on the right and pushes the page content to the side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a mode">
    Below the message box, choose **Ask** or **Autopilot** (click the mode button to switch). See [Ask and Autopilot](#ask-and-autopilot).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type a message">
    For example: "Create a workflow that emails me a summary of yesterday's orders." Press send.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What you should see:** an empty chat titled **New chat** with **Console** under it, three starter chips under **What do you want to know?** (**Teach me workflows**, **What is a workflow?**, **Beginner example**), and a box that reads **Ask about workflows, settings, or how things work…**. Under the box are a mode button (**Ask** or **Autopilot**), a model picker, a microphone and a send button. The reply streams into the panel as it is written.

<Note>
  A starter chip does not send the words on the chip. For example, **What is a workflow?** sends a longer question ("What is a workflow and how do steps connect to each other?") and renames the chat after it. The first answers are general explanations: in testing they did not mention the user's own workflows or this product's step types. Ask about your own workspace by name to get answers about it.
</Note>

In **Autopilot** mode, any action it takes (opening Workflow Studio, creating a workflow) appears in the conversation.

Autopilot knows which page, workspace, and project you are working in, so you can say "this workflow" when you have one open. It can only do what your role allows.

## Ask and Autopilot

| Mode | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Ask** | Answers questions only ("Answers only" on the button). It does not change anything |
| **Autopilot** | Can also act on the workspace ("Can act on workspace…" on the button): open pages and run setup actions |

Start in **Ask** if you want to explore. Switch to **Autopilot** when you want it to make changes. The mode button is easy to miss: a chat can open in **Ask**, so a request to create or change something gets an answer instead of an action. If that happens, switch the mode and ask again.

## What Autopilot can do with workflows

<Warning>
  **Autopilot cannot add or change steps on the canvas yet.** On a blank canvas, the **Build it** button and the example chips (**Draft → approve → send**, **Summarize and notify**, **Simple 2-step starter**) send your description to this assistant. In testing, with both **GPT-4o mini** and **Claude Haiku 5.5**, no step was added. One answer said it had "navigated to the workflow section", and the app left the canvas for the workflow list. Another said its tools "can't add nodes or connect steps" and gave a five-step plan to build by hand. While it works, the canvas card says the assistant is adding steps. Treat that as a plan, not a result. To get steps, drag them from the palette or start from a recipe. See [Workflow Studio](/workflows/studio).
</Warning>

In **Autopilot** mode you can ask it to:

- **Open** Workflow Studio, Command Center, or another page
- **Explain** how something works, or why a run failed
- **Check** a workflow for errors before you run it
- **Start, pause, resume, or stop** a run
- **Publish** a version of a workflow

The assistant's tool set also covers creating and editing workflows, but the canvas did not change in testing. Always look at what it changed before you rely on it. You can undo changes from the workflow's history.

## Conversations

The panel keeps your past conversations.

- Click **New chat** to start a fresh conversation.
- Click **Chat history** to search your conversations, reopen one, or delete one.

Conversations belong to you and to the project you were in. They are separate from workflow runs.

## Pick a model and use voice

The message box has a model picker, which lists the models your workspace can use. The chat starts on **gpt-4o-mini**. Some models appear twice, once by display name (**Claude Haiku 5.5**) and once by raw ID with a tag (`claude-haiku-5-5 [Your key]`, which uses your own key). A warning triangle marks a model with no account for the selected source. See [LLM providers](/ai/llm-providers). A microphone button starts a voice session, where you talk to the assistant instead of typing.

## Open a chat in your IDE

Once you are in a saved conversation, the top of the chat panel shows a small monitor icon named **Open in IDE**. Its tooltip says "Open this chat in VS Code / Cursor". Click it to open the same chat in your editor.

The Console asks your computer to open a `vscode://` link and shows "Opening in IDE". If nothing happens, install the AgentRuntime workbench in your editor and sign in. The Console says so in the message.

The icon is not there on a **New chat** that you have not started, and it is greyed out while a reply is being written.

## Chat with your team

The team workspace at [space.agentruntime.io](https://space.agentruntime.io) is a separate app for team channels, direct messages, and agent conversations. Use the Console panel for your own questions to Autopilot, and the team workspace to talk with colleagues and agents.

## Agents are different

**Agents** (in the sidebar) are reusable AI workers that you set up, give tools to, and use in workflows or team chat. Autopilot is the assistant that helps you operate the Console. To create an agent, choose when it replies, or debug one of its runs, see [Agents](/ai/agents). For a chat that runs one workflow run across many messages, see [Conversation sessions](/workflows/conversation-sessions).

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| I do not see **Open chat** | Look for the speech-bubble icon, the last of the three icons at the right of the top bar. If it is not there, the chat panel may not be available in your workspace. Ask [support](mailto:support@agentruntime.io) |
| The Setup guide card covers the chat box | Collapse the Setup guide with its arrow, click **Remind me tomorrow**, or click **Don't show again** to hide it for good |
| It answers but does not change anything | You are in **Ask** mode. Switch to **Autopilot** |
| **Build it** left my canvas empty | The assistant cannot add steps yet. Drag steps from the palette or pick a recipe |
| It cannot do something | It follows your role. A `project_viewer` cannot create or run workflows |
| A reply is wrong or out of date | Tell it what is wrong, or check the page yourself. Assistants can make mistakes |
