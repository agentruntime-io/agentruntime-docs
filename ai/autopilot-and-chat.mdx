**Autopilot** is the AI assistant built into the Console. You type what you want in plain language, and it can answer questions about your workspace or do the work for you, such as creating a workflow or starting a run.

This page is for anyone using the Console. You do not need any setup beyond signing in.

## Open the assistant

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the chat panel">
    In the [Console](https://console.agentruntime.io), click **Open chat** in the top bar. The chat panel opens beside the page you are on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a mode">
    Below the message box, choose **Ask** or **Autopilot** (click the mode button to switch). See [Ask and Autopilot](#ask-and-autopilot).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type a message">
    For example: "Create a workflow that emails me a summary of yesterday's orders." Press send.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What you should see:** the reply streams into the panel as it is written. In **Autopilot** mode, any action it takes (opening Workflow Studio, creating a workflow) appears in the conversation.

Autopilot knows which page, workspace, and project you are working in, so you can say "this workflow" when you have one open. It can only do what your role allows.

## Ask and Autopilot

| Mode | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Ask** | Answers questions only. It does not change anything |
| **Autopilot** | Can also act: open pages, edit workflows, and run setup actions |

Start in **Ask** if you want to explore. Switch to **Autopilot** when you want it to make changes.

## What Autopilot can do with workflows

In **Autopilot** mode you can ask it to:

- **Create a workflow** from a description, or start from a template
- **Open** Workflow Studio, Command Center, or another page
- **Edit** the workflow you have open
- **Check** a workflow for errors before you run it
- **Start, pause, resume, or stop** a run
- **Publish** a version of a workflow
- **Explain** why a run failed

Always look at what it changed before you rely on it. You can undo changes from the workflow's history.

## Conversations

The panel keeps your past conversations.

- Click **New chat** to start a fresh conversation.
- Click **Chat history** to search your conversations, reopen one, or delete one.

Conversations belong to you and to the project you were in. They are separate from workflow runs.

## Pick a model and use voice

The message box has a model picker, which lists the models your workspace can use. See [LLM providers](/ai/llm-providers). A microphone button starts a voice session, where you talk to the assistant instead of typing.

## Chat with your team

The team workspace at [space.agentruntime.io](https://space.agentruntime.io) is a separate app for team channels, direct messages, and agent conversations. Use the Console panel for your own questions to Autopilot, and the team workspace to talk with colleagues and agents.

## Agents are different

**Agents** (in the sidebar) are reusable AI workers that you set up, give tools to, and use in workflows or team chat. Autopilot is the assistant that helps you operate the Console. For a chat that runs one workflow run across many messages, see [Conversation sessions](/workflows/conversation-sessions).

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| I do not see **Open chat** | The chat panel may not be available in your workspace. Ask [support](mailto:support@agentruntime.io) |
| It answers but does not change anything | You are in **Ask** mode. Switch to **Autopilot** |
| It cannot do something | It follows your role. A `project_viewer` cannot create or run workflows |
| A reply is wrong or out of date | Tell it what is wrong, or check the page yourself. Assistants can make mistakes |
