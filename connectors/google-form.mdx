The **google-form** connector uses the [Google Forms API](https://developers.google.com/forms/api/reference/rest) with Drive. Workflows can read new responses, create and update forms, copy a template form, share forms, and set up notifications about new responses.

## Prerequisites

- A Google account that can use Google Forms and Drive.
- A Google account connection in AgentRuntime with the **Google Forms** service ticked. See [Google Workspace](/integrations/google-workspace).
- The **project_contributor** role or higher in your workspace, which lets you manage connections and MCP instances. See [Roles and permissions](/platform/roles-permissions).

## Connect in AgentRuntime

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    Go to **Connections**. In the **Accounts** section, click **Sign in with Google**. Enter a display name, tick **Google Forms** (and any other Google services you plan to use), click **Continue to Google**, and approve the permissions. If you already connected a Google account with this service ticked, skip this step. If **Sign in with Google** is missing or **Continue to Google** is disabled, Google sign-in is not set up for your workspace: [contact support](mailto:support@agentruntime.io). See [Connections](/integrations/connections).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the connector to your workspace">
    Click **MCP** in the sidebar, then **Add connectors**. Find **Google Forms** in the **App catalog**, select it, and click **Add to workspace**. The instance now appears on the **MCP connectors** page. See [MCP instances](/integrations/mcp-instances).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach your Google account to a profile">
    Open the new instance to reach **Instance config**. Under **Profiles for this connector**, click **New profile**, enter a **Display name**, and on the **Use a saved connection** tab choose your Google account under **Which connection?**. Set **Status** to **Active — can be used in workflows**, click **Save**, then click **Use this profile for workflows**. You do not enter tokens yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    In **Workflow Studio**, add the `google_form_list_forms` tool from your **Google Forms** instance (use **Browse platform MCP** if it is not listed), fill in its arguments, and run the workflow. A successful step means the connection works. Pass `page_size` `10`, `include_team_drives` `false`, and `name_contains` `""`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can build

- **Survey follow-up.** A schedule runs `google_form_list_new_responses` since the last run, an LLM tags the sentiment, and a sheet or email gets the result.
- **Application triage.** `google_form_list_responses` feeds a scoring step, and a **Human** step reviews borderline cases.
- **Recurring survey.** `google_form_copy_form` copies a master form, `google_form_rename_form` labels it, and `google_form_share_form` shares it.
- **Push notifications.** `google_form_create_watch` asks Google to notify a Pub/Sub topic when responses arrive.

## Tools

The **Required arguments** column lists the fields the tool's input schema marks as required. Some of them are optional in practice; if you have nothing to set, pass an empty value such as `""`, `[]`, `{}`, or `false`.

Tools marked **Delete** remove forms or access permanently. `google_form_trash_form` moves a form to the Drive trash instead.

### Forms

| Tool | Access | What it does | Required arguments |
|------|--------|-------------|--------------------|
| `google_form_copy_form` | Write | Copy a Google Form through the Drive API, with optional rename or parent folder. | `form_id`, `new_name`, `parent_id` |
| `google_form_create_form` | Write | Create a Google Form with initial title metadata. | `title`, `document_title`, `description` |
| `google_form_delete_form` | Delete | Permanently delete a Google Form through the Drive API. | `form_id` |
| `google_form_get_form` | Read | Get a Google Form, including info, items, settings, and revision ID. | `form_id` |
| `google_form_list_forms` | Read | List Google Forms in the authenticated user's Drive. | `page_size`, `include_team_drives`, `name_contains` |
| `google_form_move_form` | Write | Move a Google Form to a different Drive folder. | `form_id`, `add_parent_id`, `remove_parent_id` |
| `google_form_rename_form` | Write | Rename the Drive file that backs a Google Form. | `form_id`, `new_name` |
| `google_form_trash_form` | Write | Move a Google Form to Drive trash without permanently deleting it. | `form_id` |
| `google_form_update_form` | Write | Apply a Google Forms batchUpdate request to a form. | `form_id`, `requests`, `include_form_in_response`, `write_control_required_revision_id`, `write_control_target_revision_id` |

### Responses

| Tool | Access | What it does | Required arguments |
|------|--------|-------------|--------------------|
| `google_form_get_response` | Read | Get a specific submitted response for a form by response ID. | `form_id`, `response_id` |
| `google_form_list_new_responses` | Read | List form responses submitted after an RFC3339 timestamp. | `form_id`, `since_time`, `page_size` |
| `google_form_list_responses` | Read | List submitted responses for a form, with optional filtering and pagination. | `form_id`, `page_size`, `page_token`, `filter` |

### Sharing and permissions

| Tool | Access | What it does | Required arguments |
|------|--------|-------------|--------------------|
| `google_form_list_form_permissions` | Read | List Drive permissions on a Google Form. | `form_id` |
| `google_form_revoke_form_permission` | Delete | Remove a Drive permission from a Google Form by permission ID. | `form_id`, `permission_id` |
| `google_form_share_form` | Write | Share a Google Form by creating a Drive permission. | `form_id`, `role`, `type`, `email_address`, `domain`, `send_notification`, `email_message` |

### Watches

| Tool | Access | What it does | Required arguments |
|------|--------|-------------|--------------------|
| `google_form_create_watch` | Write | Create a Pub/Sub watch for form response or schema changes. | `form_id`, `topic_name`, `event_type`, `watch_id` |
| `google_form_delete_watch` | Delete | Delete a Pub/Sub watch by watch ID. | `form_id`, `watch_id` |
| `google_form_list_watches` | Read | List active Pub/Sub watches on a form. | `form_id` |
| `google_form_renew_watch` | Write | Renew a Pub/Sub watch before it expires. | `form_id`, `watch_id` |

## Example

Fetch responses submitted since the last run. `since_time` is an RFC 3339 timestamp.

```json
{
  "id": "poll-responses",
  "type": "mcp",
  "name": "Fetch new form responses",
  "tool_name": "google_form_list_new_responses",
  "tool_args": {
    "form_id": "{{input.nps_form_id}}",
    "since_time": "{{input.last_poll_time}}",
    "page_size": 100
  },
  "timeout_s": 45
}
```

## Configuration

Google connectors take no keys from you: the permissions come from your Google account connection.

**Google permissions (scopes) requested when you tick this service:**

- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/forms.body`
- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/forms.responses.readonly`
- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive`

Signing in also requests `openid` and `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email` so AgentRuntime can tell which Google account signed in.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| `google_form_update_form` fails with a revision error | Read the form with `google_form_get_form` first and pass its revision ID in the write control arguments. |
| A watch stops sending notifications | Watches expire after seven days. Run `google_form_renew_watch` on a schedule. |
| `google_form_list_new_responses` is empty | Check that `since_time` is an RFC 3339 timestamp earlier than the submissions you expect. |
| Google access stopped working | Open **Connections**, open the Google account, and click **Reconnect**. If you removed a permission, click **Update permissions** and tick the service again. |
