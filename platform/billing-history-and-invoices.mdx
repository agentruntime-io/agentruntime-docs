This page is for people who check what was charged. You will learn how to read each row in **Billing history**, what the statuses mean, and where to see what your credits were used for.

## Two lists, two questions

| List | Where | What it answers |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| **Billing history** | **Settings → Billing Overview** | What was charged to your card, and when |
| **Usage history** | **Settings → Usage & History** | What your credits were used for |

If you want to know where your credits went, use **Usage history**. If you want to know what you paid, use **Billing history**.

## Read a row in Billing history

Each row is one line with four parts, from left to right:

1. **The date.** For example "Nov 23". The year shows only when it is not this year. Hover over the date to see the full date and time.
2. **What it was.** See the table below.
3. **The amount.** This is the amount that was charged, in US dollars. A dash means the row carries no money, for example a trial starting.
4. **The status.** A coloured label, such as **Paid** or **Failed**.

If a row repeats, for example a card declined several times in a minute, the rows are folded into one with a count such as ×5.

The list shows the newest first. Use **Older** and **Newer** to move through it. With nothing to show, the list says "No payments yet. Invoices and top-ups will show up here."

### What each row is

| Row | What it means |
|-----|---------------|
| **Subscription invoice** | A charge for your plan: the first payment, a renewal or an upgrade |
| **Invoice** | An invoice that is open and not paid yet |
| **Credit top-up** | A top-up you paid for |
| **Top-up reversed** | A top-up that was refunded or taken back by your bank, and the credits removed |
| **Dispute credit** | Credits given back after a dispute was decided in your favour |
| **Payment** | Another card payment |
| **Refund** | Money returned to your card |
| **Dispute** | Your bank has opened a dispute on a payment |
| **Trial started**, **Trial extended** | The trial. No money moves. |
| **Subscription activated**, **Plan changed**, **Cancellation requested** | A change to your subscription. No money moves in this row. |

### What each status means

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Paid** | The payment went through |
| **Due** | An invoice is waiting to be paid |
| **Failed** | The payment did not go through. Nothing was charged. See [Billing troubleshooting](/platform/billing-troubleshooting). |
| **Refunded** | The money was returned |
| **Reversed** | The payment was refunded or disputed and taken back |
| **Disputed** | The payment is in dispute with your bank |
| **Void**, **Draft** | An invoice that was cancelled or not finished. Nothing is due. |

A paid invoice shows once, as **Paid**. If a payment needed a bank confirmation first, you do not see a **Failed** row for the invoice once it is paid.

A top-up where the bank step was not finished shows as **Credit top-up** with **Failed**. Nothing was charged. See [Bank confirmation (3-D Secure)](/platform/bank-confirmation).

## Invoices

Invoices for your plan are sent to the billing contact's email address. You can see and change the billing contact in **Billing contact and who pays** on **Billing Overview**. Each invoice also shows as a row in **Billing history**.

If you need a copy of an invoice or a receipt, contact [support](mailto:support@agentruntime.io) with the date and amount.

## Taxes

Today the amount in a row is the price. When tax collection starts, a row that includes tax shows a second line under the amount, such as "includes [amount] VAT" or "reverse charge". See [Taxes on your payments](/platform/taxes).

## Usage history

On **Settings → Usage & History**, **Credits remaining** shows your balance and **Usage history** lists each charge with its date, what used the credits, and the amount. It shows the credits used, not the money you paid.
