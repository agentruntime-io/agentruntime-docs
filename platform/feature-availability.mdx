This page is for anyone who cannot find a feature mentioned in the docs. It lists what is available in the Console today and what is still limited. Other guides link here instead of repeating status notes.

## Status labels

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Available** | Part of the Console for standard workspaces |
| **Preview** | Works but is limited, or may change. Not shown in the Console for every workspace. |
| **Rolling out** | Being turned on step by step. You may not see it yet. |
| **Demo only** | Works with sample data only. Not for real data. |

## How to check your workspace

- Look at the Console sidebar. Features that are not turned on for you do not appear.
- Some pages and tabs depend on your role. See [Roles and permissions](/platform/roles-permissions).
- If an API call says a feature is disabled, it is not turned on for your workspace. Contact [support](mailto:support@agentruntime.io).
