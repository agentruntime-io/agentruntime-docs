title: Bank confirmation (3-D Secure)
description: What to do when your bank asks you to confirm a card payment, and what happens if you do not finish it or it expires.
---

This page is for workspace admins who pay for AgentRuntime by card. You will learn when your bank asks you to confirm a payment, what you see in the Console, and what to do if the payment needs confirming, fails or expires.

## What a bank confirmation is

Some banks, especially in Europe and the UK, ask you to approve a card payment before they let it through. This is also called **3-D Secure**. Your bank opens a window or sends a prompt to its app. You approve the payment there.

Whether your bank asks depends on your bank and your card. AgentRuntime cannot turn it on or off.

## When it can happen

A bank confirmation can be needed when you:

- subscribe to a plan
- upgrade a plan
- start paying before a trial ends (**Start billing now**)
- pay a renewal
- pay an open invoice
- add credits

## What you see

When you start a payment that needs confirming, the Console opens the **Confirm your payment** dialog. It shows the amount and what will happen once the payment goes through:

| You were doing | What the dialog says |
|----------------|----------------------|
| Subscribing | "Your bank needs you to confirm this payment. Your plan starts as soon as it goes through." |
| Upgrading | "Your bank needs you to confirm this payment. Your plan changes as soon as it goes through; until then you stay on your current plan." |
| Paying before the trial ends | "Your bank needs you to confirm this payment. Your trial goes on until it does." |
| Paying a renewal or an open invoice | "Your bank needs you to confirm this payment to keep your subscription current." |

Click **Confirm payment**, then finish the step with your bank.

If you add credits, you do not see a separate dialog. Your bank's window opens from the **Add credits** dialog when you click **Pay**.

## Nothing happens until you confirm

Until the bank step is done:

- **Nothing is charged** and nothing is added to your workspace.
- A new subscription has not started. No plan credits are given.
- An upgrade has not happened. You stay on your current plan.
- A trial keeps going.
- Top-up credits are not added.

## If you leave and come back

If you close the dialog without confirming, the payment waits. The Console shows a notice at the top of the page and on **Settings → Billing Overview**:

> **Your bank needs you to confirm this payment.**

Click **Confirm payment** in the notice to open the dialog again. Members who are not billing admins see the notice with "Ask a billing admin to confirm it."

For a renewal, confirm soon. A renewal that is waiting for your bank is treated like an unpaid invoice, and workflow runs can be paused until it is paid.

## If the bank step is not completed

If you close your bank's window, do not finish the step, or the step fails, the Console says:

> The bank step wasn't completed, so nothing was charged. You can try again, with this card or another.

You can try the same payment again. Use **Confirm payment**, **Subscribe**, **Upgrade** or **Add credits**, depending on what you were doing. A top-up that was not completed also shows as a **Credit top-up** row with the status **Failed** in your [billing history](/platform/billing-history-and-invoices).

If your bank confirms the step but then refuses the payment, the dialog says "Your bank did not accept that payment. Enter another card to try again." Enter a different card and try again.

## If the payment expires

A payment that is waiting for your bank does not wait for ever. If you do not confirm it within about a day, it expires. Nothing is charged. When you press **Confirm payment** on an expired payment, the Console says:

> This payment expired before it was confirmed, so nothing was charged and your plan has not changed. If you still want the change, start it again.

The notice then goes away. Start the subscribe, upgrade or top-up again.

If you press **Subscribe** again while an earlier attempt is still waiting, the earlier attempt is cancelled and a new one starts. If a few attempts are made within a minute, the Console asks you to wait a minute before trying again.
