The Console adapts to narrow screens, but it is best for building on a computer. On a phone you can mostly watch runs and answer tasks.

**Who this is for:** people who approve work or check runs away from their desk.

## What works

| Area | Phone (about 375 px wide) | Tablet (about 768 px wide) |
|---|---|---|
| Navigation | A menu button opens a drawer with the same groups as on a computer. | Works. |
| **Overview** | Works. The **Setup guide** is a small bar at the bottom: tap its arrow to open it. | Works. The **Setup guide** card covers part of the lower right. |
| **Command Center** | Works. Four counters and the **In progress** list. | Not checked. |
| **Runs** | Works. Filters are behind a single **Filter runs** button. | Not checked. |
| **Workflows** | Works. The list fills the screen. Tap a workflow to open its details (**Open workflow**, **Archive**, **Share** and **Recent runs**) in a sheet at the bottom. | Works. The list is on the left, and **Open workflow**, **Archive**, and **Share** are on the right. |
| **Profile** | Works. The tabs (**General**, **Preferences**, **Security**, **Privacy**) sit in two rows, so **Privacy** is reachable. | Works. The tabs are in one row. |
| **Settings** | Not checked. | The tab list becomes a dropdown. Works. |

## Steps for a phone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Console and the menu">
    Tap the menu button to open the navigation drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put the Setup guide aside">
    On a phone the **Setup guide** starts as a small bar at the bottom of **Overview**. Tap its arrow to open it. Tap the **×** to hide it until tomorrow, or open it and tap **Don't show again** to hide it for good in this browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or check a run">
    Open **Command Center** to answer a task, or **Runs** to check a run.

    **You should see:** the counters at the top and your tasks below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Known limits

<Warning>
  Building in Workflow Studio has not been checked on a phone. From **Workflows** you can look at a workflow and its recent runs, but use a tablet or computer to build or edit.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The project switcher, which decides which project every page shows, is at the bottom of the navigation drawer under **Working in**. On a computer, it can be below the fold of the sidebar. Scroll the sidebar down to find it.
</Note>

The product tour does not start by itself on a phone (a screen under 640 px wide). On a larger screen it can start on your first visit to a workspace; close it once and it stays away in that browser. You can start it any time from **Profile**, **General**, **Product tours**.
