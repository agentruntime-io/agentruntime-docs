This page is for people who want to refer friends to AgentRuntime. You will learn how to get your referral code, what friends need to do, and where to see what you earned.

<Note>
  **Referrals** is being rolled out and is not turned on for every workspace yet. If you do not see **Referrals** in the Console sidebar, the program is not available to you yet. See [Feature availability](/platform/feature-availability).
</Note>

## How it works

- You share your personal **referral code**.
- Your friend signs up and creates **their own new workspace** with your code. Bonus workspace credits can go to both of you. The Console does not list the amounts, so check with support for the current offer.
- If your friend later pays for AgentRuntime with your code, you can earn a cash **commission**.

<Warning>
  Referral codes are for **new workspaces**. A friend who accepts an invite to your workspace does not count as a referral.
</Warning>

## Get your referral code

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Referrals">
    Click **Referrals** in the Console sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the program">
    The first time, click **Get my referral code**. This creates your promoter profile once.

    **You should see:** **Your referral code** with a **Copy code** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Send your code to friends. Tell them to enter it at checkout when they pay for AgentRuntime. You can also share the optional signup link shown on the page, which stores the code for them when they sign up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How a friend uses your code

A friend can either use your signup link, or paste the code into the **Promo or referral code** field on **Settings → Plans** and click **Apply**. Only workspace admins see that field. See [Billing and credits](/platform/billing-and-credits).

## Track your earnings

The **Referrals** page shows:

- **Pending** and **Total earned**, in US dollars
- **Confirmed commissions** and **Paid out**
- **Recent commissions**, which you can filter by **pending**, **confirmed**, **paid** or **cancelled**

Commissions are earned when a referred customer completes a qualifying payment with your code. Until then the list shows **No commissions yet**.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| No **Referrals** in the sidebar | The program is not turned on for you yet. |
| **Get my referral code** fails | Try again later or contact [support](mailto:support@agentruntime.io). |
| A friend did not get bonus credits | They must create a new workspace after signing up with your code, not join an existing one. |
| No commissions yet | Your friend has not completed a qualifying payment with your code. |
