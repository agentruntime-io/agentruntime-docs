This page is for people who want to refer friends to AgentRuntime. You will learn how to get your referral code, what your friend needs to do, and when rewards are paid. The reward amounts and limits are in [Plans and credits](/platform/plans-and-credits#current-values).

<Note>
  **Referrals** is being rolled out and is not turned on for every workspace yet. If you do not see **Referrals** in the Console sidebar, it is not available to you yet. See [Feature availability](/platform/feature-availability).
</Note>

## How it works

- You share your personal **referral code**, or the signup link that carries it.
- Your friend signs up with it and creates **their own new workspace**.
- When your friend makes their first payment, you both get a reward in platform credits.

<Warning>
  Referral codes are for **new workspaces**. A friend who accepts an invite to your workspace does not count as a referral.
</Warning>

## Get your referral code

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Referrals">
    Click **Referrals** in the Console sidebar. The **Invite a friend** part is at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your code">
    The first time, click **Get my referral code**. This creates your code once.

    **You should see:** the message "Your referral code is ready", and **Your referral code** with a **Copy code** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the signup link">
    Copy the **Signup link** and send it to your friend. The link fills in your code when your friend signs up. You can also send the code itself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What your friend does

Your friend needs to:

1. Sign up using your **signup link**, so your code is carried through signup.
2. Create **their own new workspace**.

<Warning>
  A friend referral code cannot be added later on the billing page. The Console says: "Friend referral codes are applied when you create a workspace, not on the billing page." Your friend must use the signup link when they sign up.
</Warning>

## When rewards are paid

- **Rewards are paid after your friend's first payment**, not when they sign up. The payment has to be at least the minimum shown in [Current values](/platform/plans-and-credits#current-values).
- Rewards are credited once a week.
- Both you and your friend receive **platform credits**.
- Rewards expire after the time shown in [Current values](/platform/plans-and-credits#current-values).

Some limits apply:

- A friend is rewarded only once, however many workspaces they create.
- You cannot use your own code.
- One code can reward only a limited number of friends. After that the code says it "has reached its limit".

If a payment is refunded or lost in a dispute before the reward is credited, the reward waits for the next payment that goes through.

## Promo codes

Workspace admins can open **Have a code?** on **Settings → Plans**, type a code into **Promo or referral code**, and click **Apply**. If the code is valid, the Console shows a preview of what it gives, for example "Promo code applied: [name] will apply when you subscribe."

- Codes cannot be changed on an active subscription.
- A code that is not valid shows "That code isn't valid. Check it and try again."
- You cannot use your own code.

## Partner program

The **Referrals** page also has a **Partner program** part, for people who send customers to AgentRuntime and want to be paid. Partners are approved one by one.

The partner program is not open right now. The page says "The partner program is not open right now." The terms, rates and payouts are not described in these docs. Nothing in the program is payable to you unless AgentRuntime has approved you as a partner.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| No **Referrals** in the sidebar | It is not turned on for your workspace yet. |
| **Get my referral code** fails | Try again later or contact [support](mailto:support@agentruntime.io). |
| A friend did not get a reward | They must use your signup link and create a new workspace. The reward is paid after their first payment, and only once for each person. |
| "Friend referral codes are applied when you create a workspace" | The code can only be used at signup. Use the signup link. |
| "You can't use your own code" | A code cannot be applied by the person who owns it. |
