title: Free trial

---

This page is for workspace admins. You will learn how to start a free trial, what you get, and what happens when it ends. The trial length, credits and limits are in [Plans and credits](/platform/plans-and-credits#current-values).

## What the trial gives you

- The trial is on one plan. The plan card shows it, for example **Start trial on Pro**.
- You do not need a card to start.
- You get a set number of days and an amount of both kinds of credit. Both are in [Current values](/platform/plans-and-credits#current-values).
- Trial credits can be used for workflow steps and for hosted model calls, like any other credits.
- Trial credits end when the trial ends. Anything you added by top-up is kept.

## Start a trial

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Plans">
    Go to **Settings → Plans**. You need the workspace admin role (`tenant_admin`) or billing write access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Start trial">
    Click **Start trial on** the plan name. If a plan card does not offer a trial, it says "Paid subscription only — no trial on this plan."

    **You should see:** the message "Trial started: Your trial subscription is now active." **Billing Overview** shows the **Trial** badge and when the trial ends.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A trial can also start by itself when you create a workspace. Whether it does depends on whether your account has already used its trial.

Each person can start a limited number of trials across all the workspaces they own. If you have used them all, the Console says: "You have already used the free trials available to your account." Subscribe to a paid plan or add credits instead.

## While the trial runs

A banner shows when the trial ends. The **Billing Overview** also shows an alert about what happens next:

- With a card on file: "Your card is on file, so the plan continues when the trial ends."
- Without a card: "Add a card to keep your plan when the trial ends. Without one you move to pay-as-you-go and unused trial credits expire."

## Keep your plan: add a card

Add a card before the trial ends. See [Payment methods](/platform/payment-methods). When the trial ends, the first payment is taken from the card and your plan carries on. Your bank may ask you to confirm that payment. See [Bank confirmation (3-D Secure)](/platform/bank-confirmation).

## Pay early with Start billing now

If a card is on file, you can click **Start billing now** in the trial alert. This ends the trial and starts the paid plan straight away. If your bank needs a confirmation, the trial goes on until you confirm the payment.

## When the trial ends without a card

- The plan ends and the badge changes to **Trial ended**.
- Unused trial credits end.
- Top-up credits are kept.
- The workspace keeps running while it still has top-up credits. With none left, new runs are refused until you add credits or subscribe.

The alert then says: "Your trial has ended and you are on pay-as-you-go, without trial credits. Subscribe to a paid plan, or add credits when you need them." Click **Subscribe with card** to subscribe.

See [Subscribe, change plan and cancel](/platform/subscribe-and-change-plan#after-a-plan-or-trial-ends).
