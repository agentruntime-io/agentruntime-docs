This page is for workspace admins and finance teams. It says what is true about tax on AgentRuntime payments today, and what will change when tax collection starts.

<Note>
  Tax collection is built but is switched off. This page describes only what is true today, and then what you can expect once it is switched on. It is not tax advice. Ask your own accountant how tax applies to your business.
</Note>

## What is true today

- Prices are in US dollars.
- The Console does not add tax to your payments. A subscription, a renewal or a top-up is charged at the price shown, with no tax line.
- The Console does not ask for your billing country or a VAT number.
- Billing history shows the amount charged and no tax line.

## When tax collection starts

When tax collection is switched on, AgentRuntime will add tax where the law requires it. This page will be updated before that starts.

What you can expect:

- **Tax is added on top of the price** where it applies. It is shown as a separate line, for example at checkout and on your invoice.
- **We will ask for your billing country** and postal code before a payment, so we can work out the tax.
- **Businesses will be able to add a VAT number.** It is checked, and what it changes on your invoice depends on the rules that apply to you.
- **Billing history** will show the tax that was part of a payment on a second line under the amount.
- **Credits are based on the price before tax.** Tax is never turned into credits.

This page does not list tax rates or where AgentRuntime is registered for tax. They depend on where you are and will be on your invoice.

## Questions about tax

For invoices, VAT numbers or a tax question, contact [support](mailto:support@agentruntime.io). Legal terms: [Billing and Credits Policy](https://agentruntime.io/legal/billing-and-credits-policy).
