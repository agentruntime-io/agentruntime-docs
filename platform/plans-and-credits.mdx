## Credits in one minute

AgentRuntime counts usage in **credits**. When a workflow runs, it uses credits from your workspace. If a kind of credit you need runs out, new runs or model calls that need it are refused until you add credits or move some from the other kind.

Credit balances are shown in US dollars.

## The two kinds of credit

A workspace has two kinds of credit. Each one pays for a different thing.

| Kind | What it pays for | How it is used up |
|------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Model credits** | AI model calls that AgentRuntime pays the model provider for (hosted models) | At the model provider's price for the tokens you use |
| **Platform credits** | What AgentRuntime itself does, such as running workflow steps and other platform actions | At the price of each action |

Some things to know:

- A run needs platform credits. A call to a hosted model also needs model credits. A run that does both uses both.
- If you use **your own model provider key**, the provider bills you for the model. AgentRuntime does not charge model credits for those calls. The platform part of the run still uses platform credits.
- Anything a model provider charges AgentRuntime for on your behalf uses model credits. Everything else AgentRuntime does uses platform credits.
- Hosted models can be used on every plan, and during a trial. The only requirement is that you have model credits left.

## Where credits come from

Every credit you hold is either model credit or platform credit.

| Source | Which kind | When it ends |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| **Free trial** | Both | When the trial ends |
| **Plan** | Both. Each plan includes some of each. | At the end of the billing period. A yearly plan gives a year of credits at the start and they end when the year ends. |
| **Top-up** | You choose how a top-up is split between the two kinds | They do not expire. You keep them when a trial or plan ends and when you cancel. |
| **Referral reward** | Platform credits | After the time shown in [Current values](#current-values) |

How much each source gives is in [Current values](#current-values). To add credits yourself, see [Add credits](/platform/top-up-credits).

## Which credits are used first

Inside each kind, AgentRuntime uses credits in this order:

1. **Trial** credits
2. **Plan** credits, and referral rewards, which behave like plan credits
3. **Top-up** credits

A call never uses the other kind. Model credits are never spent on a workflow step, and platform credits are never spent on a model call.

## When a kind of credit runs low or runs out

- The Console shows **Running low** under a kind of credit that is nearly used up.
- If a run or model call needs a kind you have none of, it is refused with a message that names the kind and offers a way forward. For example: "This workspace has no model credits left for this action. Move platform credits to model credits, or add credits, in Billing."
- You can fix this by adding credits, or by moving top-up credits from the other kind. See [Add credits](/platform/top-up-credits#move-credits-between-the-two-kinds).

## What happens to credits when a plan or trial ends

- **Trial and plan credits** that are unused end when the trial ends, when the plan is cancelled and the period is over, or when the billing period ends.
- **Top-up credits** are kept.

A workspace that has no active trial or plan keeps running as long as it has top-up credits. With none left, new runs are refused until you add credits or subscribe. Your workflows, connections and data are kept. See [Subscribe, change plan and cancel](/platform/subscribe-and-change-plan#after-a-plan-or-trial-ends).

You can pay monthly or yearly. A yearly plan costs ten times the monthly price. Each plan includes the same amount of model credits and platform credits.

| **Plus** | $20 | $200 | $10 model credits and $10 platform credits | $120 model credits and $120 platform credits |
| **Max** | $100 | $1,000 | $50 model credits and $50 platform credits | $600 model credits and $600 platform credits |
