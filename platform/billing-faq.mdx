---
title: Billing FAQ
description: Short answers to common questions about plans, credits, payments, tax and cancelling.
---

Short answers to the questions people ask most. Each answer links to the page with the details. Prices and amounts are in [Plans and credits](/platform/plans-and-credits#current-values).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a credit?">
    A credit is how AgentRuntime counts usage. Your workspace has two kinds: **model credits** for AI model calls that AgentRuntime pays the provider for, and **platform credits** for what AgentRuntime itself does, such as running workflow steps. See [Plans and credits](/platform/plans-and-credits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a subscription?">
    No. You can start with a free trial, or add credits and pay for what you use. A plan adds a set amount of both kinds of credit every billing period. See [Subscribe, change plan and cancel](/platform/subscribe-and-change-plan).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I pay twice if I use my own model provider key?">
    No. If your agent uses your own provider key, the provider bills you for the model, and AgentRuntime charges no model credits for those calls. The platform part of the run still uses platform credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do credits expire?">
    Trial credits expire when the trial ends. Plan credits expire at the end of the billing period. Referral rewards expire after a set time. **Top-up credits do not expire.** The times are in [Plans and credits](/platform/plans-and-credits#current-values).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my credits if I cancel?">
    Unused trial and plan credits end when the subscription ends. Top-up credits are kept. See [Subscribe, change plan and cancel](/platform/subscribe-and-change-plan#after-a-plan-or-trial-ends).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When does a change of plan take effect?">
    An upgrade takes effect right away and you pay the prorated difference. A downgrade and a cancellation take effect at the end of the billing period. The dialog tells you before you confirm. See [Subscribe, change plan and cancel](/platform/subscribe-and-change-plan).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can change billing?">
    Workspace admins (`tenant_admin`) and people with billing write access. Everyone with project access can look at plans and usage. If a button is disabled, ask an admin.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How can I pay?">
    By card, through Stripe. Prices are in US dollars. See [Payment methods](/platform/payment-methods).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My bank asked me to confirm the payment. Is that normal?">
    Yes. Some banks ask you to approve a card payment. Nothing is charged until you do. See [Bank confirmation (3-D Secure)](/platform/bank-confirmation).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find my invoices?">
    Each charge is a row in **Billing history** on **Settings → Billing Overview**. Invoices are sent to your billing contact's email. See [Billing history and invoices](/platform/billing-history-and-invoices).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my balance go down?">
    Open **Settings → Usage & History**. **Usage history** lists each charge with its date and what used the credits. If credits ended instead of being used, they may have expired. See [Billing troubleshooting](/platform/billing-troubleshooting#where-did-my-credits-go).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is tax included?">
    Today the Console does not add tax to your payments. When tax collection starts, tax is added where the law requires it, and it is shown as a separate line. See [Taxes on your payments](/platform/taxes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund?">
    Refund rules are in the [Billing and Credits Policy](https://agentruntime.io/legal/billing-and-credits-policy). If you think a payment was a mistake, contact [support](mailto:support@agentruntime.io) with the date and amount.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add more than the top-up limits allow?">
    The limits are in [Plans and credits](/platform/plans-and-credits#current-values). If you need more, [contact us](https://agentruntime.io/contact).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does referring a friend earn me credits?">
    Yes. When a friend signs up with your link, creates their own workspace and makes a first payment, you both get platform credits. See [Referrals and affiliates](/platform/referrals-and-affiliates).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
